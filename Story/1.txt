Objective: Take back the star key from King Bowser

1. The Fork in the Mushroom Forest

The path splits ahead.
Left goes into a thick part of the forest where Fire Flowers grow wild.
Right climbs up a rocky hill that leads toward Bowser’s volcano fortress.

A Goomba scurries across the trail, trips on a root, and rolls away.
Toad calls from behind you, “Mario! Hurry before Bowser uses the key!”

a) Go left toward the Fire Flower grove
b) Go right toward the volcano fortress
